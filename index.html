-ar: é, aste, ó, amos, asteis, aron. -er / -ir: í, iste, ió, imos, isteis, ieron.

Remember the written accents in the yo and él/ella/usted forms.

🔔 Special verb: DAR

Although dar ends in -ar, its preterite follows -er/-ir endings, without written accents: di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron.
